// questions

Before you apply

What is Loopcast?

Loopcast is an AI agent that runs a small app team's social media accounts as a daily loop. Every day it makes short videos from the app's own footage, posts them to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Threads, reads the numbers the next morning, and rewrites its own rules for that account based on what worked. It is built first for app teams that already have revenue but no marketer. Loopcast is a working name; it is not related to the podcast product at loopcast.ai or the audio app at loopcast.app.

How is Loopcast different from a scheduler like Buffer or Later?

A scheduler posts content you bring and stops there. Loopcast makes the content from your app's footage, posts it, reads every post's numbers the next morning, and changes what it does tomorrow based on the result. Each platform on each account gets its own rules, learned only from that platform's numbers, because YouTube, Instagram and X reward different things. Rules are written in plain words, with the evidence and date behind every rule, and you can read, edit or veto any of them. Tying each post to App Store downloads is planned and is being tested in the pilots.

What results has Loopcast had so far?

One case so far: Maozai, a solo developer's iPhone and Apple Watch virtual pet app, on brand-new accounts. From October 20 to 26, 2026 the agent made and published about 60 posts, reached 7,110 views across YouTube, Instagram, X and Threads with $0 spent on ads, and rewrote its rules four times from the data. Installs are not proven: the App Store showed 14 first-time downloads in the 30 days to October 24, a window that starts before the run, and it cannot yet say which post brought which download.

Which platforms does Loopcast post to?

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Threads first, through their official publishing APIs. TikTok and X come next; TikTok's posting API requires an app review and X's API is paid.

Does Loopcast need my passwords?

No. Every platform is connected through its official API or its own delegation, such as a YouTube channel manager role or Meta partner access. Loopcast never asks for a password and never logs in to your browser.

What does Loopcast do on its own, and what needs my approval?

It makes videos from footage you have shared, publishes routine posts, reads the numbers, writes rules and sends a weekly report on its own. Replies and comments in your voice, direct messages, any ad spend and the first post in a new format wait for your yes.

Is the content AI-generated, and is it labelled?

The videos are cut from your real app footage and screen recordings; Loopcast writes the captions. Anything AI-generated is labelled with each platform's AI disclosure setting, and music is original or cleared for commercial use, never a platform's licensed library.

How much does Loopcast cost?

Pilots are free. After that, planned pricing is: Starter, $49 a month, 1 app, 2 platforms, 12 original videos a month, and you approve every post. Main, $299 a month, 1 app, up to 4 platforms, 30 original videos a month (one a day), with only replies, messages, spend and new formats waiting for approval. Growth, $799 a month and up, up to 3 apps and 90 videos a month. Every plan posts each video to all connected platforms at no extra cost and remakes a rejected video up to two times. Extra videos are $5 each ($4 on Growth). Prices may change before launch.

What do I get in the free pilot, and what do I need to bring?

Two weeks, 14 original videos (one a day) posted to two platforms you choose, a daily log you can read and a report at the end. You bring 5 to 10 screen recordings or clips of your app, your App Store link, and admin access through each platform's own delegation, such as a YouTube channel manager role. Afterwards you can choose a plan or stop; nothing is billed automatically.

Is Loopcast available now?

Not generally yet. Five free two-week pilots are open for app teams with revenue and no marketer; apply on loopcast.dev and the developer behind Maozai replies within a few days.
